# Review a pinned range

Read `docs/harness/WORKER.md`, the packet and the diff range only. Read-only: no init, builds or edits. Report Critical and Important findings with a reproduction, and Minor findings in one line each (they never block). Flag a home-made mechanism where a standard one fits. Up to 20 lines.
