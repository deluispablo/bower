# Data analyst

**Example Corp** · Leeds (hybrid, two days in the office) · Full time, permanent

## About the role

Example Corp makes planning software for small shops. Our data team of five
looks after the warehouse, the dashboards and the monthly reports for the
board. You will join as our third analyst.

## What you will do

- Build and look after Power BI dashboards for sales and support.
- Write SQL against our warehouse (window functions every day).
- Answer questions from the product team, with numbers and a clear story.
- Help move the monthly board report from spreadsheets to Python.

## What we look for

- Two years or more with SQL.
- Power BI or a similar tool.
- Some Python; pandas is a plus.
- Clear writing for people who do not work with data.

## What we offer

- Salary: 36,000 to 40,000 a year, depending on experience.
- 27 days of holiday plus bank holidays.
- Learning budget of 1,000 a year.
- Pension: 5 % from us.

Send your CV and a short cover letter through the form by **17 October 2026**.
First interviews are in the week of 27 October.
