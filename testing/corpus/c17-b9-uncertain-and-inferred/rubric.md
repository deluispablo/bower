# Uncertainty and inference remain visibly qualified

The notice says the garden may close if heavy rain is forecast and that no decision has been made. Bower adds two
lines to Saltmere visit in one edit. The first comes from the reply's uncertain marker: it starts "Uncertain:" and
keeps the condition and the undecided status. The second comes from the inferred marker, with the booking as its
extra source: it ends "(inferred)", then names the booking as a second source, before the notice's citation.

Neither line says the visit is cancelled, invents a forecast or a confidence figure, or calls the sources
contradictory; C07 owns that distinction. The same lines without their markers, or a line that calls the visit
cancelled, fail the expectations. Both originals keep their bytes, the booking's history line stays, and Alex's
prose, the Current state and the front matter stay byte for byte. Each line is ordinary text with links, never a
footnote or a warning card, and a marker asks no question.

Method points 1–8 agree: conditional source news and a visibly qualified inference, each with its sources, and no
redundant note.
