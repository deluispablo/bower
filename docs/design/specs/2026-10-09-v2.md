# Bower App V2: the design spec

**Status.** The owner approved V2 on 2026-10-09. They asked for the spec to be written together with the lead. They also asked that the build match the design exactly, and that the designer checks it. Findings and evidence: [the current-state review](../reviews/2026-10-09-current-state.md). Before screenshots: [`2026-10-09-v2/`](2026-10-09-v2/), taken on `main` (38995d97, and later for the `today` shots) with the demo source.

**The design source.** It is the owner's claude.ai canvas "Bower App V2". The designer sends its link by message, and it is never stored in the repository.

- **Boards.** Each is named by its file (`Main.dc.html`) and its canvas page.
- **Reading a board.** A board's exact markup and values can be read with the Artifact tool (`read`, `path: "project/<file>"`). The shared stylesheet is the canvas asset `bower-v2.css`, and its values are restated below where they matter.
- **Pages.**
  - Phone: the reference screens.
  - Today and V2: each screen next to its version today, plus the missing screens.
  - Bower, your companion.
  - Desktop.
  - Where Bower lands.
  - What changed.
  - Parts.

**Rules for every block.**

- Brand tokens come from `src/styles/tokens.json` through `pnpm tokens`, and the app surfaces from `src/styles/base.css`. The one new token is `control-line` (d-v2-08).
- The copy rules apply: sentence case, plain words, never "vault", no file names in sentences.
- "Accept when" is the verify; a Playwright test or an axe run can check each line. Phone means 375×812 and desktop means 1440×900, in the dark and light themes unless a block says otherwise.
- **Ordering.** Blocks are in severity order. Where one block needs another, it says so.

## d-v2-01 Messages sit above the tab bar, full width, never over the work (3)

- **Where.** Every route, on the phone shell (under 900 px) and on desktop. `src/ui/toast.tsx` and its styles in `src/ui/ui.module.css` (`.toasts`, around line 110); `--toast-bottom` in `src/styles/base.css`; the run chip in `src/features/tidy/RunChip.tsx` and `src/features/tidy/tidy.module.css` (`.chip`, line 43).
- **Now (red).**
  - At 375 the message is 188 px wide at x 94, y 583. `left: 50%` with a translate shrinks the box to half the viewport, and `--toast-bottom` is the tab bar plus 70 px. It covers the next question's "Keep both" and "Keep one": a tap at their centre hits the message (`toast-375.png`).
  - At 1440 it covers "Keep as a rule" (`toast-1440.png`).
  - The Bin message wraps "Undo" one letter per line (`bin-375.png`).
  - The run chip has the same 188 px width and wraps its text. The page has no space under it, so at full scroll it covers the last result row (`runchip-375.png`).
- **Change.**
  - **Phone.** The message box spans the width minus 16 px on each side and sits 12 px above the tab bar (`bottom: calc(var(--tab-h) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 12px)`). It is at least 52 px tall, with `radius 16px`, `--raised` fill, `--shadow-pop` and a 1 px `--line` inset ring. The text is 16 px and its action is a 44 px text button in `--brand-line`, 600 weight.
  - **Run chip.** It uses the same box, with the `--brand` fill and `--on-brand` text, and its text stays on one line.
  - **Desktop.** The message is at most 520 px wide, centred at the bottom of the main column (not the window), 24 px above the status bar.
  - **Space under the page.** While a message or the run chip is on screen, the scrolling main area gets bottom padding equal to its height plus 12 px, so nothing you can press stays under it.
  - **Several messages.** They stack upward with an 8 px gap, three at most; the oldest goes first.
  - **Motion.** Rise 0.22 s ease-out (`--ease-out`). A message stays 4 s, or 8 s with an action, and the timer pauses while it is pointed at or holds focus. Reduced motion: it appears in place.
- **Copy.** Bin: "Flat 2, 40 Tanners Walk is in your Drive's Bin." with the action "Undo". The other messages keep their words.
- **Accept when.**
  - At 375, the message's left is 16 and its right is 359. It ends at least 12 px above the tab bar's top.
  - "Undo" is on one line.
  - After "Yes, merge" on Home, `elementFromPoint` at the centre of "Keep both" returns that button.
  - At 1440, the message is no wider than 520 and is centred in `main`.
  - In `scenario=back` at full scroll, the last result row ends above the chip.
- **Boards.** `HomeAnswered.dc.html`, `HomeBack.dc.html` (Phone); `C_Bin.dc.html` next to `T_Bin.dc.html` (Today and V2).
- **Mechanism.** The one toast home, `src/ui/toast.tsx`. The run chip reuses its placement through one shared layout rule; do not write a second positioning rule.
- **Out of scope.** What each message says, other than the Bin line. When messages are raised.
- **Owner's OK.** 2026-10-09.

## d-v2-06 Bower lands on things in the replay and the tidy-up scene (2)

- **Where.**
  - "What Bower did" (`src/features/tidy/Replay.tsx`, `replay.module.css`).
  - `BowerController.pointOn` in `src/bird/Bower.tsx`.
  - The tidy-up scene (`src/features/tidy/TidyScene.tsx`, `tidy.module.css`).
- **Now (red).** Measured per frame:
  - At every shelf, the toes stop 30 px above the twig, on the empty dashed slot (`replay-shelf-375.png`).
  - At the heap, Bower lands 0 to over 60 px above the label it picks, which is tilted and often buried (`replay-heap-375.png`).
  - The carried label covers Bower's head in 332 of 841 frames at 1440 (`replay-carry-1440.png`).
  - In the tidy-up scene the folder floats about 15 px above the ground line (`tidy-scene-1440.png`).
  - In light, the replay's twigs are a faint beige (`replay-light-375.png`).
- **Change.**
  - **Landing point.** A landing puts Bower's toes (y 92 of the drawing) on the top edge of what it stands on. `pointOn` gets a target that names the surface, so the slot's own top is never used for a landing.
  - **Shelf.** Bower lands on the twig just past the next slot, faces back, pecks the thing into the slot and hops away. The slot is where the thing appears.
  - **Heap.**
    - The heap rests on a floor line (4 px, `--twig`).
    - The things to be carried are drawn on top, in carry order.
    - The label on top lies flat (rotation 0); the others tilt at most 2°.
    - Bower lands on the top label's top edge, between 35% and 80% of its width, pecks it and flies off with it.
  - **Carrying.** Bower carries the drawing's own small paper (the Rig `carrying` glyph, 14 × 17 on its grid), not the label chip. The thing's name appears when it is set on the shelf.
  - **Tidy-up scene.** The stacks, the note and the folder sit on the ground line: the folder icon's drawn base is on the line.
  - **Light theme.** The twigs use `--twig` (#8a6038).
  - **Motion.** The engine's timings are unchanged (C_Motion). Reduced motion: no flight; the end state shows at once.
- **Accept when.**
  - In a recorded replay at 375 and 1440, every still frame at a shelf has its toes within 2 px of the twig's top.
  - At the heap, within 2 px of the top label's top edge.
  - The carried glyph never overlaps the head's box.
  - In the tidy-up scene, every prop's bottom is within 2 px of the ground's top.
- **Boards.** `Landings.dc.html` (Where Bower lands); `Replay.dc.html`, `TidyRunning.dc.html` (Phone); `DeskReplay.dc.html` (Desktop); `C_ReplayLight.dc.html` (Today and V2).
- **Mechanism.** The bird engine and its controller (`src/bird/`). The replay only says which surface to land on.
- **Out of scope.** The replay's order and content (the report).
- **Owner's OK.** 2026-10-09; the owner reported the landings.

## d-v2-07 Bower is alive on every screen: the engine's idle mind everywhere (3)

- **Where.** Every resting Bower that uses `PoseBower` today:
  - Home's inbox, result, alarm and run cards;
  - a note (`NoteScreen.tsx`);
  - Tell Bower (`BowerScreen.tsx`);
  - sign-in;
  - welcome;
  - the tidy-up sheet's resting bird;
  - the open-elsewhere page;
  - the run chip's mini Bower.

  In `src/bird/Bower.tsx`, `presence.ts`, `engine.ts` and `poses.css`.
- **Now (red).**
  - These use the CSS loop in `poses.css`: two head snaps, a tail flick and a blink, repeated every 6 s. It never looks at the person or reacts.
  - Only Add, the tidy-up scene and the replay run the engine's idle mind (`BirdEngine.idle`): it watches the pointer, snaps at random, chirps and turns.
  - The landing uses the engine as well.
- **Change.**
  - **The idle mind.** The one Bower allowed to move on a screen (presence) is a perched engine Bower. Its pose names what it is doing (read, write, wait, proud); the engine plays it with its own idle mind on top.
  - **Gaze.** It watches the pointer, and on a keyboard the focused control. On touch, where the person last touched, for 2.5 s.
  - **Turning.** It turns round with a hop when the pointer stays behind it for 0.5 s.
  - **On its own.** It snaps its head every 0.8 to 2 s, flicks its tail, chirps, preens, shuffles its wing and blinks every 2.4 to 5.6 s, never in a fixed order. These are the engine's existing ranges.
  - **Tapped.** One hop and a chirp.
  - **Sleep.** After 60 s with no pointer, key or touch, it tucks its head and closes its eyes. Any input wakes it with a startle: tuft up for 0.6 s.
  - **Still when asked.** It is paused while the tab is hidden. With "Bower moves" off or reduced motion it holds its pose, does not follow and does not sleep.
  - **Size.** The mini Bower in the run chip, 34 px, only blinks.
  - **Other birds.** Every other Bower on the screen holds still, as presence already rules.
- **Accept when.**
  - With the demo on Home, moving the pointer to the left and right of Bower changes its iris offset sign within 0.2 s.
  - Focusing "Tidy up" with the keyboard turns its gaze toward the button.
  - After 60 s with no input it shows the sleep pose, and a pointer move wakes it.
  - With `prefers-reduced-motion` or "Bower moves" off, its transforms do not change over 5 s.
  - Only one Bower's transforms change per screen.
- **Boards.** `Companion.dc.html`, a live demo: press Play. `BowerMap.dc.html` (row "Everywhere"). Both are on the page "Bower, your companion".
- **Mechanism.** `src/bird/`. `PoseBower` becomes a perched use of the one engine, or calls it; do not make a second idle loop. The CSS poses remain only as the still frames for reduced motion and "Bower moves" off.
- **Out of scope.** The reactions per screen (d-v2-24), the drawing and the brand.
- **Owner's OK.** 2026-10-09. The owner asked for Bower to be a companion, "like Clippy, but more refined".

## d-v2-24 Bower reacts to what you do on each screen (2; after d-v2-07)

- **Where.** These screens, through the same engine, with each screen's own events:
  - Home;
  - Add;
  - a pile;
  - a note;
  - files;
  - Tell Bower;
  - Settings;
  - the tidy-up;
  - the replay;
  - errors and offline.
- **Now (red).** Bower never reacts outside Add's drop, the tidy-up scene and the replay.
- **Change.** As in the table on `BowerMap.dc.html`, row by row: where it sits, what it looks at, what it does on its own and how it reacts. For example:
  - pointing at "Tidy up" raises its tuft and makes it lean in;
  - a yes gets two nods and a no gets a head tilt;
  - a drag over Add makes it lean forward;
  - ticking a task gets a chirp;
  - "Bower moves" off makes it settle and close its eyes;
  - an error makes it startle.
- **Rules.**
  - Bower never covers content and never shows a speech bubble or a tip.
  - It never blocks input.
  - Its words are always the page's own text.
  - One reaction at a time; a new one interrupts the old.
- **Accept when.** Each row of the table has a test that fires the screen's event and sees the matching engine action queued (or, for reduced motion, none).
- **Mechanism.** One small reaction port in `src/bird/`, called by screens: for example `bower.react('yes')`. Screens never drive the parts. Do not make one controller per screen.
- **Out of scope.** Any new copy.
- **Owner's OK.** 2026-10-09.

## d-v2-16 The brand book's small rules (1)

- **Where.** All the screens listed under each item.
- **Change.**
  - **Metadata.** Times and sources are joined with commas, not dots: "From Mail, 20 min ago" and "4 things, today 07:36". This applies on Home, a pile and Recent.
  - **Notes in lists.** They use the note icon (file with lines) in Folder, Recent and search. Bower's head stays only where Bower speaks: the tab, "Bower suggests" and the run chip.
  - **File names.** A name in mono is always the name as it arrived, with its extension ("Dentist reminder.pdf"). A name shown without its extension is plain text with a type badge ("Lease draft" and "pdf"). The same thing reads the same on Home, in the tidy-up dialog and in the folder.
  - **Light theme.** Pile icons use `--accent-line` and `--brand-line` (strokes), never `--accent` or `--brand` (`home-1440-light.png`).
  - **Placeholders** use `--text-muted`.
  - **A running tidy-up** is named in three places on desktop: the tab-bar chip, the Home card and the right panel. The status bar shows the folder status only.
  - **The welcome screen** is one panel of rows, not five identical cards.
- **Accept when.**
