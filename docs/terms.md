_Last updated: 2026-09-27._

This page is the terms of service for this Bower instance. It is written for the person using the app, not for lawyers, and it covers the same ground as [the privacy page](/privacy) from a different angle.

## What Bower is

Bower is [open-source software](https://github.com/deluispablo/bower), released under the MIT licence. There is no company behind it and no contract between you and anyone: what you are using is a private copy of that software, deployed and run by the operator described below.

## Who runs this instance

This particular copy of Bower is run by an operator — the person who invited you to use it, most likely for a small group such as their household. The operator is not a company. If you have a question, ask the person who invited you.

## Your account

You can only sign in if the operator has added your email address to this instance's invite list. At any time, from Settings, you can sign out, revoke this instance's access to your Google account, and delete your account, which removes everything the instance stores about you. The operator can also remove you from the invite list, which signs you out and stops it acting on your behalf.

## Your content

Your notes stay in your own Google Drive, in the folder you chose. You own them; Bower never claims any right over them and never copies them into its own storage. See [the privacy page](/privacy) for exactly what this instance can see and store about you.

## Acceptable use

This instance is for your own notes. Do not use it for anything unlawful, and do not feed it other people's personal data without their consent — it is built for one person's own notes, not as a place to process someone else's.

## Availability and warranty

Bower is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind. It runs on free tiers of third-party services and may stop working, change, or be taken down without notice. Nobody guarantees it will be available, or that an automated tidy-up run will produce accurate or useful results. Keep your own backup of anything in your Drive that matters to you.
