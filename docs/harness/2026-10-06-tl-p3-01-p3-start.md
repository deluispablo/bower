## Evaluation hygiene (from the P2 close)

A service worker cached an old build on a reused port. That made a replay read `body` everywhere, and explains the unreproduced F1/F2 tool failures. Every evaluation therefore:

- runs on a fresh origin (a new port, or cleared site data);
- records the served bundle name, and checks it against the build before reading any result.
