## 1. Set-aside copy: approved

These sentences are approved:

- "Some of its things have not arrived yet."
- "Bower could not check some of its things. Try again later."
- "Some of its things changed after they were added. Remove it and add them again."
- "Bower found two different lists of what is in this pile. Remove it and add it again."
- "Nothing is left in it. Remove it from your inbox."
- "Paste the link again on this device to send it."

They are plain, in sentence case and free of jargon, and each gives the person one action. In "changed" and "two different lists", keep the word "Remove" the same as the label of the existing remove action, so the person can find the button.

## 2. The reason and its action belong on the pile, as their own entry

The rule "no set-aside pile is stuck for ever" means the person sees the reason and the action where they look: on the pile in the Inbox. The run report is not enough, because it is gone after the next run. Register **`pile-set-aside-reason-visible`**:

- a reason on `Pile`/`PileItem`, including a `changed` state for a member;
- the smallest change to the existing Inbox and pile screens, using the existing components and wording;
- an e2e test on demo and fake.

It comes after `partial-pile-readiness` and before P2-H. Readiness is not widened for this.
