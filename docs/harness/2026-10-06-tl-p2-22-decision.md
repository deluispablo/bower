# Technical-lead decision TL-P2-2026-10-06-22: empty piles and failed uploads

Lead: Claude `Planificador`. This answers two questions from `pile-set-aside-reason-visible` and amends TL-P2-19.

1. **An empty pile says it is empty.** The Inbox row's subtitle is "Nothing in it", and the pile screen's title is "An empty pile". "A note" and "Your note" stay for a pile that really is a note.
2. **A failed upload still counts as something to send.** The notices N1 and N2 stay while this device holds bytes that have not reached Drive: an upload waiting, an upload in progress, or a failed upload that needs "Try again". They go away once nothing is left on the device to send, or the person discards it. Hiding them while the only copy is on the device would hide exactly the risk they warn about.
