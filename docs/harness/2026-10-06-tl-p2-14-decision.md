# Technical-lead decision TL-P2-2026-10-06-14: single-session details

Lead: Claude `Planificador`. This answers the five questions in the coordinator's single-session plan (`docs/harness/2026-10-06-single-session-plan.md` on the integration branch). The plan's shape is accepted:

- a place per tab, kept in `sessionStorage`;
- a lease per person, `{holder, epoch, beatAt}`, in the fake Worker;
- `session(place, check|take|leave)` next to `me()`;
- Web Locks plus `BroadcastChannel` within the browser;
- a fail-closed fence in the bound Drive proxy and on `process`/`createFolder`;
- the runner left unfenced;
- sub-units S1, S2a and S2b.

It applies TL-P2-13. Adopted revision stays `2026-10-05-contracts-2`.

1. **TTL 3 minutes, heartbeat 30 seconds: accepted.** A live lease always asks: "Use here". An expired lease means no other session is alive, so the new place takes it without asking, as WhatsApp opens directly when no other window is open. A closing tab tries to release with `leave` on `pagehide`; the TTL is the fallback. An offline holder that comes back after expiry finds the lease lost. It shows the block screen, and its outbox waits.
2. **`POST /session {place, action: 'check'|'take'|'leave'}` → `{active, epoch}`: approved as a P3 obligation.**
   - `check` by the holder refreshes `beatAt`.
   - `take` raises the epoch.
   - `place` is a random id per tab and carries no secret. The Worker binds the lease to the authenticated person.
   - The Worker enforces the epoch on its own endpoints: `process`, `createFolder` and `deleteAccount`. A stale epoch gets one plain error.
   - Drive writes go straight from the app, so the app fences them itself. That limit is accepted (TL-P2-13).
3. **P3: a short cache of the lease, not a call per write.** The app trusts a lease it confirmed less than 5 seconds ago. It checks again before the outbox flush, before each upload starts and before `process()`; the Worker checks that last one too. Within one browser, `BroadcastChannel` makes a takeover immediate. A write in that window is the same accepted limitation as a Drive write already in flight.
4. **Yes: the lease key includes the source, and the scenario for fake.** Demo, each fake scenario and real are separate worlds and never block each other. For real, the key is the authenticated person, not an email address.
5. **Yes: a blocked place stops polling `status()`, its heartbeat and every background job.** The block screen covers the content, so nothing stale shows as current. "Use here" takes the lease and then reloads the data in full, because what was on screen may be out of date.
