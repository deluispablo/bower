## Contract

1. **One declaration home.** `src/data/contracts/note-templates.ts` declares the five shapes, consumed by note-format and generated-note planning; it is not another serializer. Sections:
   - Source: Summary, Key points, Related, Transcript; original/url; the body is immutable after creation; an absent url is omitted, never invented.
   - Concept: Current state, Notes, Related, Timeline.
   - Project: Goal, Next steps (`- [ ]`), Current state, Notes, Timeline; due only from evidence.
   - Area: Standard to maintain, Current state, Notes, Timeline.
   - MOC: Overview, Notes, Sources; reuses B1a's moc-overview, moc-figures and moc-members regions.
2. **Ownership.** Goal and Next steps are Bower regions in note-edit (Q5). A new Source or MOC gets no generated Timeline; legacy Timelines are kept unrewritten. Person notes never gain Bower metadata or template sections.
3. **Front matter on Bower notes:** type/status, ≤200-character summary (B2a format), ≤3 tags, created, updated, source, related, plus template keys, through note-format only. Each new note links its hub/MOC and 1–5 relevant notes; the hub counts among them; no unrelated padding.
4. **Compatibility.** Old notes and their sections keep their meaning; legacy serializer output stays readable. Compatibility rows for every contracts commit.
