## C08 promise split into checkable parts

1. **Useful authorship:** a report or long answer addresses the requested decision, with the outcome, reasoning, limits and practical next steps; a document of extracted facts alone is insufficient.
2. **Tables and comparisons:** valid Markdown, one row per intended subject, aligned requested columns, source-grounded facts and explicit missing values; prose explains the trade-off without invented scores or calculated facts.
3. **Sources:** each material claim and table row traces to the actual admitted or stored source; internal links resolve and external addresses are only those supplied or lawfully obtained. The source documents remain intact.
4. **Durable delivery:** one named document in the right existing folder, reachable from the result, with correct created/updated reporting and truthful request completion.
5. **Revision:** revise the same generated document by stable identity, change the requested content, preserve unrelated facts, personal blocks, citations and dated history, and record one truthful revision.
6. **Completeness and safety:** retain all necessary prose and the end of the document; cut input or output is never presented as complete. Apply normal read-to-write fences, replay without duplicates, and preserve prior bytes through the existing recovery/undo mechanism.
