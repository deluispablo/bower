## Contract (contracts only; no runtime table writer yet)

1. **`.bower/type-table.json`** is versioned and hidden. It is written only through the existing `write-runner-json` effect and its validators, with no new effect kind.
   - Rows have stable slug ids, and the first match wins.
   - There are 8 seed rows: TL-P4-38's 7 plus the cover letter. Accepted rows go before the generic fallbacks.
   - Strict write. Lenient read: an invalid row is skipped, and an unreadable file means the seed rows are used and nothing is written that run.
2. **Property values are bounded:**
   - text, at most 200 characters;
   - list, at most 10 items;
   - enum, at most 20 values;
   - finite numbers;
   - money as an amount plus an ISO 4217 currency;
   - ISO dates.
   The row declares a `table` subset of at most 4 columns. The technical keys (type, status, summary, tags, created, updated, source, related) are never columns. The row may also declare `group_by` (Q2), either an enum or a date by year.
3. **Proposals (Q3).** An immutable `.bower/proposals/<id>.json` with its exact payload, behind the question subject `proposal`. It is stored with the question, and the answer carries only its id.
   - At most 2 new distinct payloads per run, deduplicated by payload; at most 5 open at once.
   - A declined payload never returns. An ignored one stays open.
   - No round cap.
   - Reuse Q1a's question and answer homes; never a second store.
4. **Profiles (Q4).** A new Bower region kind in ownership-format and note-edit, with its own mark, at most 400 grounded words. The short Current state stays separate. A note the person wrote is never adopted.
5. **Compatibility.** Old notes, questions, manifests and checkpoints keep their meaning. Compatibility rows go in for every contracts commit.
