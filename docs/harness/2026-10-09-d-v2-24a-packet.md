## Contract

The governing ruling is **TL-P4-26** (`2026-10-09-tl-p4-26-d-v2-24a-contract.md`, main 8c3af2d6), with **addendum 1** (the designer, main 2f1ae3bb). Where this packet and the ruling differ, the ruling wins. The contract review is `2026-10-09-d-v2-24a-contract-review.md`. This entry starts **after the d-v2-07 startle round merges**, because it reuses the one startle step.

1. **One port.** `src/bird/react.ts` holds the `REACTIONS` table and `react(kind, at?: Element): { end(): void }`.
   - It reaches the bird with the turn through `activeHandle()` and calls only `play(kind, at)`.
   - `at` becomes a frame point through the gaze's frame rect.
   - Screens never drive parts. There is no controller per screen.
2. **Any bird.** A reaction plays on any pose.
   - While it plays, it owns the act channel and pauses the pose script through `poseActed`; the script resumes after.
   - A perched carry keeps what it holds.
   - Write one red per pose class: minded, working, and perched layer.
3. **One composite act per kind.**
   - Each kind is one makeAct with its own keyed timeline of steps (look, startle, nod, chirp…), never a chain through `S.queue`. There is no second queue.
   - A new reaction cuts the running one at the next frame (within 0.1 s, which is 6 fake frames).
   - The startle is one step type: d-v2-07's wake plays the same step.
4. **One pending slot.** `S.tap` becomes `S.pending: { kind: 'tap' | ReactionKind; at? } | null`, and the latest call wins.
   - While a layer bird is in flight or carrying, the port sets `pending`, and it plays on landing.
   - A reaction cuts a perched carry pose.
   - Taps go through `react('tap')`.
5. **Types.** `ReactionKind` is its own type, outside `WEIGHTS` and `draw()`, so a reaction never fires on its own. `act(name)` stays the mind's and the tests' path.
6. **Moves off and on.** useBirdEngine owns the engine's lifetime.
   - **Off:** `react('moves-off')` runs. The engine and its handle are kept until the settle ends on engine time. Then `release()` runs and the CSS still frame shows.
   - **On:** the engine is created, then it wakes and shakes.
   - **Reduced motion:** no settle; the still frame shows at once.
   - **Layer birds** settle the same way and keep their engine.
   - Only `moves-off` on Settings closes the eyes: eye 1 → 0.08 and head 8° down over 0.6 s, ease-out, held while that bird is on screen. Every other bird, layer birds included, eases to its pose's still frame in 0.6 s with its eyes open.
7. **Sustained kinds** (drag-over, point-tidy) hold until `end()`, the 10 s cap on engine time, or pointerleave, dragleave or drop, whichever comes first. 24a tests `end()` and the cap; 24b wires the events.
8. **Sleep.**
   - **Mind sleep:** a reaction wakes the bird with the startle, then plays the reaction. It counts as input: it marks recency and restarts the 60 s.
   - **The sleep pose:** only `error` and `offline` wake it: the startle, then the alarm pose while the message shows, then back to sleep. Every other kind is ignored.
9. **`react('error')`** is the startle and a look. The alarm pose stays the screen's.
10. **Values (addendum 1 (E)).** Each kind is a `REACTION_REFERENCE` row, with ranges at the value ±2°. Every value eases in with no overshoot past 2°. Addendum 20's meta-test enforces a row for every kind.
