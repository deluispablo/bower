## C10 promise split into checkable parts

1. **Consent:** the run's allowWeb gates all search, fetch and revalidation calls; request words and page content never grant permission.
2. **Actual lookup:** a permitted request receives only results from the fake lookup in tests and the ruled real provider in production; distinguish retrieval from a saved result.
3. **Accurate citations:** every web-derived claim has the exact read source address and inspectable evidence supporting that claim; reject invented addresses and unsupported facts.
4. **Freshness:** distinguish publication, applicability, retrieval and previous check dates; recently fetching an expired page does not make its claim current.
5. **Reuse:** preserve provenance and the original check date when reusing evidence; no duplicate notes/history or assertion that a saved result was freshly fetched.
6. **Honest outcomes:** success, fetch failure, stale evidence and denial have distinct observable answers; incomplete evidence never becomes a current fact or silent state replacement.
7. **Durable effects:** record a successful answer only after its promised writes land; replay, failed writes and person edits retain history and do not misreport success.

Parts 1–3 and 6 have bounded fixture proposals below; applicability intervals are fixture facts. A general age threshold, cache policy or retry limit needs a ruling, not a number selected by this draft.

## Open product questions for the lead and designer

There are **six** open questions; this draft decides none.

1. **Freshness (lead, designer for wording):** what makes evidence current for different questions—page applicability, publication age, a person's “check again,” or a defined age limit—and how should undated evidence be described?
2. **Reuse and consent (lead):** may Bower answer from saved web evidence with web off, and when web is on which saved results can avoid retrieval or revalidation; what happens to a run's captured permission if Settings changes while it runs?
3. **Citation presentation (designer with lead):** should a successful answer and Timeline link directly to the source page, to a kept source note, or both, and where do checked/publication/applicability dates appear without clutter?
4. **Stale or conflicting evidence (lead with designer):** should old evidence be retained only as a dated reference, appended as explicitly stale history, or left untouched, and should a conflicting current page trigger a C07 question rather than choose a band?
5. **Failure and retry (lead with designer):** is stale/failed/denied a completed request with an honest answer, or waiting for a retry; what bounded retries, partial-result wording and person action should exist?
6. **Lookup initiation and disclosed scope (lead with designer):** only explicit lookup requests or also tidy-up research when the switch permits it, which request data may leave the folder, and how does the person see what was checked and reused?

The delivered stale no-write outcome and denied sentence are proposed acceptance semantics for review, not rulings on automatic retention or request settlement. Changes to these policies require the lead to revise the cases before packets.
