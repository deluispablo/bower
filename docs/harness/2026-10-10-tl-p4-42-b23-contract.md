# Technical-lead decision TL-P4-42: the B2 and B3 contract (summaries and hot)

These are the rulings on the 8 questions in Codex's B2+B3 preparation draft (p4-b23-prep 9794af58, `2026-10-10-b23-prep.md`). The draft is accepted as the base, with its entries B2a, B2b, B2c, B3a and B3b. It follows TL-P4-39 and its addenda, TL-P4-41, and the owner's principle of designing for normal use with one general mechanism.

**What needs the owner's OK.** Nothing here. Summaries and hot are content Bower writes, and hot is hidden (TL-P4-41 Q11). Visible questions stay with Q1.

## Rulings

- **Q1. One summary bound for every route.**
  - The existing companion bound applies to every note Bower creates: one line, not empty, no control characters, at most 200 characters.
  - When the model writes the note (companion, long words, comparison), the summary travels in the same execution reply. WordsShape gains an optional `summary` under the same validator. There is no new model call.
  - When no summary is authored (short words, a saved link, the scripted fallback), a script takes the first sentence of the body and cuts it at a word boundary within 200 characters, with "…". Only the summary is cut, never the body.
  - The scripted fallback for an unknown source keeps its honest wording: it describes what the file is, never what it might say.
- **Q2. Which notes carry a summary, and when it changes.**
  - Required on every note Bower creates (R2). Mechanism files (`index.md`, `hot.md`, `log.md`, `.bower/`) keep their own heading formats with no front matter. Rules keeps its generated count and its custom-summary exception. A note the person wrote never gains metadata, including on C05's append and the link-rewrite.
  - There is no backfill scan. A summary changes only inside an edit Bower already makes to its own note's Current state, as the same single edit with the same validator and inverse. A Bower note that has no summary gets one then. That is B2c: kept, escalated, and narrowed to this one case. Source notes are immutable and never change.
- **Q3. Hot's sections, all by script, at most 300 words.**
  - Open threads: every open question first (authoritative open questions), then proposals once B5 and Q1 exist. At most 10 rows, then "and N more waiting on Home".
  - Current focus: Bower's project and area hubs with a settled Timeline change in the last 30 days, newest first, ties by path, at most 5 rows. Never modifiedTime, and never the model's opinion.
  - Recent changes: the last 10 settled changes from the journal, newest first.
  - A word is a whitespace-separated run. When the total is over 300, rows are cut from Recent changes first, then Current focus, with "(N more)". Open threads are cut last. Empty sections show nothing, with no padding.
- **Q4. Reading hot.** Confirmed as proposed. Hot is read after the token, manifest and done checks and before the semantic context reads. It sits at position 6 of the prompt order (TL-P4-39 §1.5). It is capped at 4,000 characters with an explicit cut mark. A missing hot adds nothing. B1d owns the prompt builder, and B3 only supplies the text.
- **Q5. Maintenance target, and no archive for script views (narrows TL-P4-39 §1.1).**
  - Archive-before-rewrite protects views a model writes: About me and MOC Overviews. A view rebuilt whole by a script from primary evidence (hot, the index, MOC figures and members) can always be rebuilt, so it is **not archived**. There is no `.bower/archive/hot/`.
  - B3b adds root `hot.md` as one more target of B1a's single `maintain` effect and to RUNNER_WRITES. Its input versions live in maintain's persisted declaration and checkpoint, the same place as the index's. There is no other store.
  - Hot is written only when anything other than its date line changed, which is the index's rule.
- **Q6. Thread inputs.** Confirmed: the existing open questions are the only source now. B5 and Q1 add their identities when they land, and the proposal list is empty until then. Hot never holds consent or starts a continuation, and every actionable item reaches Home.
- **Q7. Lint stays in B8.** Missing-summary detection and its display belong wholly to B8, through the Bower health note. B2 does not score lint.
- **Q8. Lifecycle.** Confirmed. Hot refreshes on every run that settles effects or has changes in the feed, including answer-only, instruction-only and no-item runs. A failure before the plan writes nothing, and a run that is already done writes nothing. Undo rebuilds hot and the index from the restored evidence. Outcomes are recorded per artifact through B1a's effect lifecycle and op ids. Any generic gap goes back to B1a; B3 adds no second checkpoint protocol.

## Order and overlap

- **B3a (the pure hot reduction)** has no overlap and may start now in a free Codex slot.
- **B2a (the scalar format, escalated)** waits for C05, which also edits `note-format.ts`. It goes before B1e, or after B1e's handoff.
- **B2b, B2c and B3b** follow the B1 owners' handoffs of the shared files, as the draft lists them. B3b no longer needs an archive target, so it is escalated only for the hot target in RUNNER_WRITES.

Cases 1-4 are accepted. Cases 5, 8, 9 and 10 are added in their entries; case 5 drops its archive step. Cases 6 and 7 go with B2c and B2b. Each entry follows TL-P4-29: a red from a Codex red session, the implementation, a Codex pass, and a fresh Claude review.
