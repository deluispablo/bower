# Technical-lead decision TL-P2-2026-10-06-36: a pile note in a format Bower refuses

Lead: Claude `Planificador`. This answers the finding behind `pile-note-format-visible`, and applies TL-P2-08 and TL-P2-11.

A pile whose note Bower refuses to parse stays listed in the Inbox. The causes are a future `bower_format`, a damaged boundary and invalid encoding. Such a pile is held from runs, and its note is read-only.

Retrying cannot change a format, so the reason offers Remove (recoverable, TL-P2-31) rather than Try again:

- **Unsupported version:** "This pile's note needs a newer Bower."
- **Damaged boundary or invalid encoding:** "Bower can't read this pile's note."
