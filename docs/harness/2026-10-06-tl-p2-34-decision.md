# Technical-lead decision TL-P2-2026-10-06-34: set-aside rows, context restore copy and note-only piles

Lead: Claude `Planificador`. This answers lane b-dest and amends TL-P2-32 items 1 and 3.

1. **Two rows when both fail: option (3), with no contract change.** Under the owner's simplest-approach rule, two honest rows are better than a new materialization rule. If an item's move and its note both fail, the report shows both rows. Each is true, and they do not contradict each other. "At most one row" in TL-P2-32 item 3 is withdrawn. The single-row cases stay as ruled:
   - **Move applied, note not written:** the `filed` row plus "Bower couldn't write its note this time."
   - **Move rejected:** the move's row, as below.

   **The sentence for a rejected move depends on why it was rejected:**
   - **A conflict** (the precondition found it moved, renamed or removed) keeps the specific sentence "It was moved, renamed or removed before Bower could file it."
   - **Any other rejection,** including a failed dependency, uses "It couldn't be filed this time."

   This applies to every item, saved links and merges included. It refines R4-3 m-c. When the cause is known, the specific sentence is honest; when it is not, the general one promises nothing.
2. **Context restore: keep "Some of its things have not arrived yet." with Try again.** From the person's point of view the context note is one of the pile's things, and Try again runs `restoreContext`. No new sentence.
3. **Binning the first note of a note-only pile: ratified.** The `bin` is recoverable and `dependsOn` the `create-text` that wrote its words. Without it, the next run would write the same words again. Binning the leftover input is what legacy already does for a pile. The other pile notes stay, under TL-P2-32.
