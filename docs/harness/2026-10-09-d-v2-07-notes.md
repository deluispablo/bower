## Round 5 (addendum 7)

- Mechanism (2a008c4f): `HeadProfile` + `follow()` in `src/bird/engine.ts`, the online trapezoid of robot motion
  control (Lynch and Park, Modern Robotics, 9.2.2, http://hades.mech.northwestern.edu/index.php/Modern_Robotics;
  online form as in Ruckig, https://github.com/pantor/ruckig). State: angle and velocity. Target: act channel `H.t`
  plus gaze `H.gaze`; a target write never touches the state. Discrete braking is exact
  (s + (s - a) + ... + (s - n a) = |d|), so a move lands with no overshoot and no step in speed.
- Classes: `glide` (returns, gaze, poses, acts) 6 deg/frame, 1.5 deg/frame^2; `snap` (the `snap` act, the startle's
  rise) 6 deg/frame, no acceleration cap, lands on its target. Both caps sit `DRAWN` = 0.02 under the bound: the
  drawn angle has two decimals, so a measured speed change can read up to 0.02 over the profile's. The red bounds
  are unchanged; flag for the lead if the margin should be ruled differently.
- removed: the `head` and `gaze` springs; the startle's keyed head and `startleFrom.head`; `easeGaze`, `settleT`,
  `settleFrom`, `settleTo`, `gazeWant`; the gaze's boolean return; `pin(C.head)` in preen (now a profile target).
- Reds: the addendum 6 property (seed 2610, 40 runs) passes; meta-test, startle, gaze-return, every pose loop pass.
