- **Acceptance contract:**
  - **`POST /session`** (`requireSameOrigin`, `requireSession`, JSON body) takes `{ place, action: "check" | "take" | "leave", gen? }` and answers `{ active, epoch }`.
    - `place`: 1–64 characters from `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`.
    - `gen`: a non-negative safe integer; a missing `gen` counts as 0.
    - A body that fails validation answers 415 `unsupported-media-type`, as the JSON guard does. Use no other code.
  - **Lease semantics.** Match `FakeLease.session` exactly, in the person object, keyed by the authenticated `sub`, inside one synchronous storage section with no `await` between read and write:
    - TTL is 3 minutes (`LEASE_TTL_MS`).
    - A holder's check renews it with the same epoch, even after expiry when nobody took it (TL-P2-25).
    - A free or expired lease is granted to whoever checks, with epoch + 1.
    - `take` always moves the lease, with epoch + 1.
    - `leave` by the holder frees it and keeps the epoch.
    - A check by the place that left, with nobody in between, gets the same epoch back.
  - **Leave generation** (TL-P3-14). The Worker stores each place's last `leave` generation.
    - A `check` whose `gen` is lower than that place's last leave is discarded: it grants and renews nothing, and answers the current `{ active, epoch }` for that place.
    - A missing `gen` counts as 0, so a place with any recorded leave has its gen-less check discarded.
    - The fake lease does the same. `src/data/session.ts` sends its `leaveGen` on every `check` and `leave` through `LeaseCall`, `WorkerHttp.session` and the fake Worker.
    - Contract tests, one on each side:
      - Worker: `a check started before the place's latest leave is discarded`.
      - Fake: `the fake discards a check started before the place's latest leave`.
  - **Schema v6:** the lease's beat time, the place that left, and a per-place leave generation (its own small table keyed by place, or columns, your choice with a comment).
