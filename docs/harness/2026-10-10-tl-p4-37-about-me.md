# Technical-lead decision TL-P4-37: About me grows (capability C17)

**Owner, 2026-10-10:** "About me is one of the main features, and the one we must take most care of." It should grow as Bower ingests files and as the person adds rules and more. Bower needs this context to know what to do with the person's notes, what to look up on the web and how to link notes to each other.

**Today:**
- The Worker creates `About me.md` from the folder template when it sets up the folder.
- The app protects it from being moved, renamed or binned (vault-contract.ts:85).
- No capability writes it.
- The planner is not shown it.

The owner's test on staging (account B, with Claude off) also showed it empty.

## Rulings

1. **A new capability, C17, comes right after C05 and before C06.** Connections (C06), disagreement (C07), rules (C11), documents (C08), queries (C09) and web research (C10) all depend on knowing the person, so it goes first. It is escalated, because it is a protected note and personal context.
2. **It is one home, Bower's own section of `About me.md`.** The person's own text in the note is never touched. Each fact Bower learns is one line with its source and date, in the timeline-line.ts grammar. The facts cover:
   - who the person is and what they are working towards (for example a planned move abroad);
   - their projects and areas;
   - their preferences, and how they like things filed and written.
3. **The person is in control.**
   - A fact the person removes (in Drive or through the app) is never written again from the same source.
   - A change of fact (a new city, a new job) replaces the line and cites the newer source; it never overwrites silently.
   - Facts in sensitive categories (health, beliefs, finances beyond what the person filed for a project) are written only when the person's own words state them. Bower never infers them from a document.
4. **It feeds every run.** The planner always sees About me, bounded and placed first in the context. Every slice's prompt uses it: where to file, which notes are related, what is worth looking up.
5. **What it learns from:**
   - ingested files: a fact about the person, never about third parties;
   - the person's pile words and requests;
   - rules;
   - answers to questions.

   Each new fact is reported in the run result ("Learned about you: …") with an Undo.

## Order

1. Codex drafts the C17 preparation now, in a free slot: corpus cases with expected outcomes, scripted proposals and harness gaps, all with made-up data. The owner's real staging test is never copied.
2. The designer is consulted on how the section, the report row and the Undo read.
3. The lead reviews the preparation. Then the packet, the Codex contract sign-off, the red from a Codex red session, and the implementation (Codex). Claude bower-escalated is the fallback, and every escalated step follows TL-P4-29.
4. The owner's own test is repeated on staging with Claude on, once C05 and C17 have landed.
