# Technical-lead decision TL-P4-26: the d-v2-24a contract (the react() port)

**Input:** the [contract review](2026-10-09-d-v2-24a-contract-review.md) (claude/p3 3f477a5a; 12 Important, 5 Minor), spec d-v2-24 with bower-map.md, and TL-P4-25 and its addenda. The premises are as the review cites them at d1477d72. The UI points (1, 3, 5, 7 and 11) take the designer's answers, recorded as addendum 1. Until then, the defaults below stand.

## Rulings

1. **Which bird a reaction plays on.** A reaction plays on any bird, the working poses included. While it plays, it owns the act channel and pauses the pose script, through the existing `poseActed` path (engine.ts:956); the script resumes after it. A perched carry keeps what it holds. There is one red per pose class: minded, working, and layer perched.
2. **One composite act per kind.** Each reaction kind is a single composite act: one makeAct with its own keyed timeline of steps (look, startle, nod, chirp…). It is never a chain through S.queue, and no second queue exists.
   - The startle is one step type. The wake from d-v2-07 plays the same step (`S.startleT` folds into it), so there is one startle.
   - A new reaction cuts a running one at the next frame.
3. **"Bower moves" off and on.** useBirdEngine owns the engine's lifetime.
   - **Off:** it calls `react('moves-off')`, keeps the engine and its handle until the settle ends (engine time; fake frames in tests), then calls release(), and the CSS `posed <pose> still` frame shows. The settle eases each part to that pose's still frame.
   - **On:** it creates the engine, then plays wake and shake.
   - **Reduced motion:** no settle; the still frame shows at once.
   - **Layer birds** settle the same way and keep their engine, as they do today.
4. **Signature:** `react(kind, at?: Element): { end(): void }`. The port turns `at` into a frame point through the same frame rect as the gaze (Bower.tsx:142), so a screen passes an element and never drives parts.
   - **Kinds with a target:** bin (Undo), error ("Try again"), rule (Your rules) and duplicate (the line).
   - **Leaning toward the pointer** uses the gaze's current pointer.
5. **Sustained kinds** (drag-over, point-tidy) hold until `end()` or a 10 s cap in engine time, whichever comes first. `react('error')` is the startle and the look only; the alarm pose stays the screen's. Tests cover Add's dragleave and drop and TidyButton's blur in 24b; 24a tests `end()` and the cap.
6. **One pending slot.** `S.tap: boolean` becomes `S.pending: { kind: 'tap' | ReactionKind; at? } | null`. It is one field, and the latest call wins, whether a tap or a reaction.
   - The port never calls the act path while a layer bird is in flight or carrying. It sets `pending`, which plays on landing.
   - A reaction cuts a perched carry pose, as a tap does (TL-P4-25 addendum 2 (4)).
7. **Values** come from the designer's table (addendum 1). Each kind gets a `REACTION_REFERENCE` row with the designer's values, its ranges are those values ±2°, and addendum 20's meta-test enforces a row for every kind.
8. **Types and the port's only call.**
   - `ReactionKind` is its own type, kept out of `WEIGHTS` and `draw()`, so a reaction never fires on its own.
   - The port calls only `play(kind, at)`. `act(name)` stays the mind's and the tests' entry, and the port never calls it.
9. **Reds that can fail:**
   - every "does nothing" test has a positive control: the same engine with motion on, and the same kind moving the rig;
   - "a sleeping bird ignores reactions" has its own test;
   - "each kind plays its action" samples the parts through readRig, as Bower.test.tsx:223 does;
   - "starts within 0.1 s" is 6 fake frames, measured from the react() call to the first changed target.
10. **The real entry point:**
    - the port through `activeHandle()` on a mounted PoseBower in Bower.test.tsx;
    - the tap going through `react('tap')`, so real input exercises the port, with an e2e on Home;
    - BirdLab.tsx, in scope, gets a reactions row as the designer's test page.
11. **Sleep.**
    - **Mind sleep:** a reaction wakes the bird. It counts as input: it marks recency and restarts the 60 s.
    - **The sleep pose:** follows the designer's answer (default: reactions are ignored, except error and offline, which wake it with the startle).
12. **Files in scope:**
    - src/bird: react.ts (the port and the `REACTIONS` table) and its test, engine.ts, Bower.tsx (useBirdEngine), presence.ts (the handle held through the settle), pose-reference.fixture.ts, Bower.test.tsx, engine.test.ts, BirdLab.tsx;
    - e2e/bird-engine.spec.ts.

    Not touched in 24a: ui/report.ts, ui/online.ts, the screens and prefs; those belong to 24b. The settle and the wake run on fake frames, never on a real wait (`c3-timer-gate`).

The entry stays on Opus and escalated, with a Codex pass. It is dispatched after the designer's addendum and sign-off, and there is no contract pass 3.
