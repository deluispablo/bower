# Technical-lead decision TL-P2-2026-10-06-25: coming back to an expired lease nobody took

Lead: Claude `Planificador`. This answers the coordinator's UX question about `single-active-session` and amends TL-P2-14 §1.

**Ruling.** A place whose lease expired takes it again silently, without asking "Use here", when nobody took the lease in the meantime: the lease's holder is still this place and its epoch is unchanged. This covers a phone left in the background or a laptop asleep for more than 3 minutes. The place just refreshes `beatAt` and continues. Its epoch stays the same, because no other place wrote with another one.

If any other place took the lease after it expired, even briefly, the epoch has moved on. The returning place then shows the block screen, as before. This is what WhatsApp Web does: it asks only when another window really used the session.

**P3.** The real session contract (headers, CORS, the 409 `stale-session` answer, `leave` with `keepalive`, `DELETE /me` releasing the lease) is recorded in [P3_DRAFT](../P3_DRAFT.md) as an obligation.
