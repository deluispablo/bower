# Technical-lead decision TL-P2-2026-10-06-19: set-aside reasons in the Inbox

Lead: Claude `Planificador`. This answers the copy and UX questions from `pile-set-aside-reason-visible`, and amends TL-P2-11 and TL-P2-15 where noted.

1. **"Changed" as the line under a member that changed: approved.** The word is already used elsewhere in the app, and the pile's sentence explains it.
2. **Actions per reason:**
   - **Not arrived.** A "Try again" button: approved.
   - **Could not check.** Add a "Try again" button that checks again now, rather than leaving the person at a dead end. The sentence becomes "Bower could not check some of its things. Try again.", which amends TL-P2-11.
   - **Changed, two lists or empty.** "Remove this pile" in the Inbox, with a confirmation. On the pile screen, the sentence points to the existing Remove button. Approved.
   - **The old alert.** "Some things did not reach your Bower folder." stays for piles without a declaration only. Approved.
3. **Demo sample pile: approved.** It is labelled "A pile set aside", its text is "Bank letters for the mortgage", and it holds one changed statement. All of it is invented demo copy.
4. **The storage notices show only while this device still has something to send.** This applies to "storage refused" (N2) and to "couldn't keep a copy" (N1). Each notice goes away once nothing is left to send, without waiting for a reload. This amends TL-P2-15.
