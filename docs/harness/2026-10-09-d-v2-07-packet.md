## Contract

1. **The idle mind on the perched bird.** The one Bower with the turn runs the engine's idle mind on top of its pose script. Use v2-perched-bower's hook, its `look` source and the injected scheduler; add no second idle loop.
2. **Gaze.**
   - The bird follows the pointer.
   - With a keyboard, it follows the focused control (`focusin`).
   - On touch, it follows the last touch point for 2.5 s.
3. **Turning.** It turns with a hop when the pointer stays behind it for 0.5 s.
4. **Idle acts.**
   - Every 0.8 to 2 s it does one of: head snap, tail flick, chirp, wing shuffle, preen. The order is never fixed.
   - It blinks every 1.6 to 5.2 s, and one blink in four is a double. These are the engine's existing ranges (`engine.ts:571,615`).
   - It never preens or snaps while following input, meaning input within the last 2.5 s.
5. **Preen (new in the engine).** The head turns down to the wing, about 38°, for 0.9 s with two small dips, then back. The wing shuffle (−10° for 0.22 s) stays a separate act.
6. **Tapped.** One hop and a chirp (`poke()`).
7. **Sleep and wake.**
   - After 60 s of engine time with no pointer, key or touch input, the bird tucks its head and closes its eyes.
   - Any input wakes it with a startle: tuft up for 0.6 s.
8. **Still when asked.**
   - The bird pauses while the tab is hidden.
   - With "Bower moves" off or reduced motion, it holds its pose: no following and no sleep.
   - Reduced motion is subscribed through `matchMedia` change events, not read once per render (`Bower.tsx:12-16`).
9. **Size.** The run chip's 34 px bird is minor (v2-perched-bower). It only blinks, and never takes the turn.
10. **Other birds hold still,** as presence rules.
11. **Second-way risks:**
    - no idle loop of its own in `PoseBower` (rAF, timers or CSS);
    - no second presence registry;
    - the 60 s runs on the engine clock, never a real timer.
