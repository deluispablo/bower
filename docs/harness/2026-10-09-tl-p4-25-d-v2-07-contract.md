# Technical-lead decision TL-P4-25: the d-v2-07 contract (Bower's idle mind)

**Input:** the [contract review](2026-10-09-d-v2-07-contract-review.md) (claude/p3 e8227603; 14 findings, 8 Important), spec block d-v2-07, and TL-P4-22 addenda 6-9 and 14-17. The premises are as the review cites them at 9816dfe1. The UI points (1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10) take the designer's answers, recorded as addendum 1. Until then, the defaults below stand.

## Engineering rulings

1. **Two channels, one owner each.** There is no second idle loop. `engine.idle()` (engine.ts:796) is the one mind, and `perch()` (:703-792) is the one pose script.
   - **The gaze channel:** the iris ix/iy and a small, clamped head offset added on top. The mind always owns it.
   - **The act channel:** head, tail, jaw, tuft and wing targets. The pose script owns it in the working poses: read, write, carry, proud, land and alarm. The mind owns it in the resting poses, idle and wait, where its random acts replace the fixed CSS-era loop and use the same angles (designer default 1).
   - Each channel writes only its own parts. The gaze offset is added after the act channel, never written over it.
2. **The range test stays enforced** (addenda 14-17). The nine-pose test runs the engine with the mind on, with no input (the gaze is neutral) and with the mind's acts paused through one engine option, `acts: false`, which is a test seam.
   - The working poses keep their CSS-range checks unchanged.
   - idle and wait are checked act by act: the rest frame within 1 px, and each act's peak within its CSS range plus 2°.
   - A separate test with the acts on checks the timing: an act every 0.8-2 s, and the blink range.
3. **The mind's options:** `{ sleeps, acts }`. Perched birds get `sleeps: true`. Layer birds (Add, Replay, TidyScene) and the landing get `sleeps: false`. Gaze, preen and startle apply to every bird (designer default 2). The e2e and qa tests of the layer scenes must stay green.
4. **The run chip** follows the designer's answer. Default: its still frame, no engine and no second loop.
5. **Sleep reuses the sleep pose's targets,** with no second sleep frame. apply() reads one `asleep` flag, set either by the sleep pose or by the mind's 60 s rest, in place of `pose === 'sleep'` (:940). The mind sleeps only from idle or wait. A bird whose pose is sleep follows the designer's answer (default: it stays asleep).
6. **The engine API:**
   - `look(p, source)`, where source is pointer, focus or touch;
   - `nudge()`, which wakes the bird with a startle;
   - the existing `poke()`;
   - the recency of the last input, kept in engine time.

   The listeners live in useBirdEngine's look source (addendum 6(5)): pointermove, pointerdown, keydown and focusin, the last using the control's rect. PoseBower has none. A mouse that stops moving lets the bird sleep after 60 s.

7. **Tap.** A pointerdown listener on the perched box, which never calls preventDefault or stopPropagation. The box gets `aria-hidden`. While the engine is busy, a poke is queued as one and dropped after 1 s (designer default 5). Enter and Space never poke.
8. **Reds and guards.**
   - `preen and snap never run while input is recent` gets a positive control: the same seed with no input does preen and snap.
   - Tests that pass today are labelled guards.
   - The tab-order test asserts the box, not the svg.
   - The notes name which reds fail today.
9. **Test seams:**
   - getBoundingClientRect is stubbed for the control and the frame;
   - a PointerEvent polyfill goes in the test setup;
   - the reds mount PoseBower alone in Bower.test.tsx with fake frames, not "the demo on Home".
10. **Values** come from the designer's answer (default 6). The old random `turn()` (:834) goes. The behind-the-pointer turn after 0.5 s stays.
11. **Reduced motion and "Bower moves".** There is one home, prefs.ts: a `useSyncExternalStore` over the matchMedia change event, so flipping the query mid-render stops the engine. A test flips it. prefs.ts is in scope.
12. **Files in scope:**
    - src/bird: engine.ts, Bower.tsx, presence.ts (only if the chip changes), pose-reference.fixture.ts;
    - src/app/prefs.ts;
    - Bower.test.tsx, engine.test.ts, presence.test.ts;
    - e2e/bird-engine.spec.ts;
    - the notes.

    poses.css's still frames are untouched. Nothing waits on real time.

13. **The clock.** Sleep counts engine time, and a hidden tab does not count toward it. A tab that is shown again resumes with no startle. A test checks "paused while hidden".
14. **e2e.** One measure: sample the transform of every `[data-bird]` descendant at 0 s and 2 s on phone and desktop. Exactly one bird changes, the minor bird is exempt only if the designer gives it a blink, and the test uses polls, never a fixed wait.

The minors are folded in: the preen's gaze moves the iris down and back, and neither preen nor startle sets B.girth.
