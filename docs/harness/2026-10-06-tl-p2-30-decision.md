# Technical-lead decision TL-P2-2026-10-06-30: a pile folder that left the Inbox, and an unreadable declaration

Lead: Claude `Planificador`. This answers the questions from `declared-pile-folder-lost` and `declaration-read-isolation`. It replaces TL-P2-29 (2) and the limit recorded in TL-P2-08 (a).

## A. A pile folder moved or deleted outside Bower is the person's decision

**Ruling.** Retire `declared-pile-folder-lost` as superseded by this decision, not passed. Bower does nothing when a declared pile's folder leaves the Inbox other than through Bower:

- moved elsewhere in Drive;
- hard-deleted;
- purged from the Bin after 30 days.

The pile simply stops showing in the Inbox. Its declaration stays in `.bower/piles` as inert history. With no live folder it is never shown, and no run captures it.

**Why.**

- **It cannot be detected reliably.** A client cannot tell a lost folder from one that ended normally. Drive lists only live files, Remove and legacy write no disposition, and the Bin is purged after 30 days ([Google Drive Help](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/14933051)). Any rule based on the folder's absence would bring removed piles back as false ones about a month later.
- **The standard for sync clients is the same.** Drive for desktop, Dropbox and Obsidian on a synced folder all treat a move or delete made outside the app as the person's intent, and follow it. They do not resurrect anything.
- **Nothing is lost by Bower.** The person's own action decided it.

The implementer's option 1, a folder still live but moved out of the Inbox, is also left out. Moving a pile into a project folder is a deliberate act, so Bower should not process it there.

## B. `declaration-read-isolation`

- **(a) Confirmed.** A single damaged declaration copy shows the "could not check" reason with Try again, because failed reads and malformed records are treated alike (TL-P2-08 (a)). Copies that really differ, and a declaration that names another folder, keep the "two different lists" reason.
- **(b) A closer sentence for this case:** "Bower could not read the list of what is in this pile. Try again." The broader "Bower could not check some of its things. Try again." stays for members that cannot be checked.
