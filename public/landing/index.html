Why a bowerbird?

Bowerbirds live in Australia and New Guinea. They build a bower of twigs, collect bright things, mostly blue, and set them out by size in front of it. Move one and they put it back. That is the job Bower does for everything you save.

Throw it all in. Tap once.

Save things as they come, from your phone or computer. When you are ready, tap Tidy up: Bower reads what it can, names each one for what it is and files it in your Drive, in folders by how soon you need things (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive), unless your rules say otherwise. Try it here.

Bower is a side project about a second brain that does the boring part for you: the brand, the bowerbird and the code. Bower is one drawing with nine poses and a small motion engine, so it hops, flies and snaps its head like a real bird. It runs on free tiers with no servers, it was designed and coded with Claude Code, and its filing runs on Claude.
